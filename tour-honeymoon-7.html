7 DAYS OF DISCOVERY

Day by day

Explore each day’s experiences, stops and overnight destination.

01DAY 1Airport to KandyOvernight: Kandy

Route: Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) to Kandy

Highlights: Romantic welcome, private air-conditioned transfer, scenic hill-country gateway, Kandy arrival, hotel check-in and relaxation.

Begin your forever with a warm welcome from your Loving Lanka Tours chauffeur guide right at Bandaranaike International Airport. Step into your private air-conditioned vehicle and enjoy a comfortable transfer towards the cultural heart of Sri Lanka, Kandy. Travel through scenic countryside and lush green landscapes, setting a romantic tone for your holiday. Arrive in Kandy, check into your carefully selected hotel, and unwind after your flight.

Stay · Kandy
02DAY 2Explore KandyOvernight: Kandy

Highlights: Royal Botanical Garden in Peradeniya, Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, Kandy culture and romantic evening.

After breakfast, begin your first full day together with a romantic morning walk through the Royal Botanical Garden in Peradeniya, famous for its landscaped lawns, towering palm avenues, and vibrant orchid house. Afterwards, visit the sacred Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, one of Sri Lanka's most cherished spiritual landmarks situated beside tranquil Kandy Lake. Spend the evening relaxing together or exploring local artisanal crafts.

Stay · Kandy
03DAY 3Kandy to Nuwara EliyaOvernight: Nuwara Eliya

Highlights: Central highlands transition, Ceylon tea plantation and factory tour, tea tasting, Ramboda waterfall views, Nuwara Eliya "Little England".

After breakfast, leave Kandy and journey deep into the breathtaking central highlands. Watch the scenery transform into rolling green valleys blanketed with world-famous tea estates. Stop at a working tea plantation and factory to discover how Ceylon tea is hand-picked and crafted, followed by a romantic tea-tasting experience. Admire the cascading Ramboda Waterfalls before arriving in Nuwara Eliya, affectionately known as "Little England" due to its cool climate, misty air, and charming colonial-era architecture.

Stay · Nuwara Eliya
04DAY 4Nuwara Eliya to EllaOvernight: Ella

Highlights: Scenic hill country, misty mountain vistas, optional scenic train ride, romantic evening in Ella.

Following breakfast, embark on one of the most scenic drives in Asia towards the laid-back mountain haven of Ella. Travel past cascading waterfalls, deep gorges, and endless green tea terraces. For couples wishing to experience the world-famous hill-country railway, a scenic train journey through the mountains can be arranged, subject to ticket availability. Arrive in Ella, check in to your mountain retreat, and enjoy a cozy evening surrounded by nature.

Stay · Ella
05DAY 5Ella to South CoastOvernight: South Coast Beach

Highlights: Nine Arch Bridge, Little Adam's Peak viewpoint, Ravana Waterfall, transition to the tropical southern coastline.

Start your morning with a romantic visit to the iconic Nine Arch Bridge, hidden amidst lush jungle and tea fields. Enjoy a gentle, leisurely walk up Little Adam's Peak for panoramic valley views, and stop to admire the majestic Ravana Waterfall. Afterwards, leave the cool mountains behind and travel south toward the warm, golden shores of the Indian Ocean. Check in to your beach resort and watch the sunset side-by-side.

Stay · South Coast Beach
06DAY 6South Coast & Galle FortOvernight: South Coast / Galle

Highlights: Tropical beach leisure, UNESCO-listed Galle Fort exploration, historic ramparts, romantic candlelit or beachside dinner.

Spend a relaxing morning soaking up the tropical sun on the golden southern beach. In the afternoon, travel a short distance to explore the historic UNESCO World Heritage site of Galle Fort. Wander hand-in-hand through atmospheric cobblestone streets lined with Dutch-colonial architecture, boutique artisan shops, art galleries, and trendy ocean-view cafes. Conclude your day with a romantic sunset walk along the ancient ramparts, followed by a special celebration dinner by the sea.

Stay · South Coast / Galle
07DAY 7South Coast to Airport

Highlights: Final morning by the ocean, private airport transfer, departure.

Enjoy a leisurely breakfast overlooking the ocean and cherish your final moments together under the tropical sun. Your Loving Lanka Tours chauffeur guide will pick you up from the hotel and provide a comfortable, private air-conditioned transfer directly to Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) in time for your international flight. Your romantic Sri Lankan honeymoon journey comes to a close, leaving you with memories to treasure for a lifetime.

Airport drop-off included.

Your itinerary can be tailored to your pace and flight times. Optional activities, train journeys and marine excursions depend on availability, operating schedules and suitable conditions. Wildlife sightings vary.
