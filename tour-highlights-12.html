12 DAYS OF DISCOVERY

Day by day

Explore each day’s experiences, stops and overnight destination.

01DAY 1Arrival in Sri LankaOvernight: Negombo / Colombo

Route: Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) to Negombo / Colombo

Highlights: Welcome to Sri Lanka, private transfer, hotel check-in, optional short visit around Negombo or Colombo.

Welcome to Sri Lanka! Meet your Loving Lanka Tours chauffeur guide at Bandaranaike International Airport and begin your holiday. Transfer to your hotel and relax after your flight. Depending on your arrival time, you may enjoy a short visit around Negombo or Colombo.

Stay · Negombo / Colombo
02DAY 2Negombo / Colombo to SigiriyaOvernight: Sigiriya / Dambulla

Route: Negombo / Colombo to Sigiriya / Dambulla

Highlights: Cultural Triangle transit, countryside scenery, authentic village life, optional traditional lunch.

Travel towards Sri Lanka’s Cultural Triangle. Enjoy beautiful countryside scenery and experience authentic Sri Lankan village life along the way. Optional village experiences and traditional Sri Lankan lunch can be arranged.

Stay · Sigiriya / Dambulla
03DAY 3Sigiriya & DambullaOvernight: Sigiriya / Dambulla

Highlights: UNESCO World Heritage Sigiriya Rock Fortress, ancient gardens, fortress ruins, Dambulla Cave Temple.

Visit the spectacular Sigiriya Rock Fortress, one of Sri Lanka’s most famous historical attractions. Explore the ancient gardens, fortress ruins and surrounding landscapes. Later, visit the magnificent Dambulla Cave Temple, home to beautiful Buddha statues and ancient paintings.

Stay · Sigiriya / Dambulla
04DAY 4Sigiriya to KandyOvernight: Kandy

Highlights: Spice and herbal garden, Ayurvedic traditions, Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, Kandyan cultural performance.

Travel towards the cultural capital of Sri Lanka. Visit a traditional spice garden and learn about Sri Lanka’s famous spices and Ayurvedic traditions. Continue to Kandy and visit the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic. In the evening, enjoy a traditional cultural performance if available.

Stay · Kandy
05DAY 5Explore KandyOvernight: Kandy

Highlights: Royal Botanical Garden Peradeniya, Kandy city tour, local culture, handicrafts, gem shopping.

Discover the highlights of Kandy. Visit the Royal Botanical Garden in Peradeniya, explore Kandy city and experience the local culture. There will also be time to discover Sri Lankan handicrafts, gems and local shopping.

Stay · Kandy
06DAY 6Kandy to Nuwara EliyaOvernight: Nuwara Eliya

Highlights: Central highlands, tea-covered mountains, waterfalls, tea plantation and factory tour, Nuwara Eliya ("Little England").

Travel through the spectacular central highlands. Enjoy breathtaking views of tea-covered mountains, waterfalls and valleys. Visit a tea plantation and factory to learn how world-famous Ceylon Tea is produced. Continue to Nuwara Eliya, known as Little England.

Stay · Nuwara Eliya
07DAY 7Nuwara Eliya to EllaOvernight: Ella

Highlights: Hill-country landscapes, scenic viewpoints, waterfalls, optional scenic train journey.

Continue through Sri Lanka’s beautiful hill country towards Ella. Enjoy scenic viewpoints, waterfalls and mountain landscapes along the way. A scenic train journey can be arranged subject to railway operation and ticket availability.

Stay · Ella
08DAY 8Explore EllaOvernight: Ella

Highlights: Nine Arch Bridge, mountain views, Ravana Waterfall, relaxed customization options.

Enjoy a relaxed day discovering Ella. Visit the famous Nine Arch Bridge, enjoy spectacular mountain views and visit Ravana Waterfall. The day can be customized with easy sightseeing options for guests who prefer a relaxed experience rather than hiking.

Stay · Ella
09DAY 9Ella to Yala / UdawalaweOvernight: Yala / Udawalawe

Highlights: Wildlife region transfer, Yala National Park or Udawalawe National Park safari, elephants and diverse wildlife.

Travel towards Sri Lanka’s wildlife region. Depending on your selected route, enjoy a safari experience at Yala National Park or Udawalawe National Park. Look out for elephants, crocodiles, deer, monkeys and a variety of beautiful birds.

Stay · Yala / Udawalawe
10DAY 10Wildlife Safari & South CoastOvernight: South Coast / Unawatuna / Galle

Highlights: Morning jeep safari, transition to the tropical southern coast, beach hotel relaxation.

Enjoy an early morning wildlife safari. After breakfast, continue towards the tropical southern coastline. Relax at your beach hotel and enjoy the beautiful ocean surroundings.

Stay · South Coast / Unawatuna / Galle
11DAY 11Galle & Southern CoastOvernight: Galle / Unawatuna / Bentota

Highlights: Historic Galle Fort, colonial architecture, narrow streets, boutique cafés, ocean views, free time at the beach.

Explore historic Galle Fort, with its charming colonial buildings, narrow streets, cafés, boutiques and ocean views. Continue along the southern coastline and enjoy free time at the beach. Optional experiences such as a boat ride, turtle experience or local village visit can be arranged.

Stay · Galle / Unawatuna / Bentota
12DAY 12South Coast to Colombo / Airport

Highlights: Final morning in Sri Lanka, Colombo sightseeing and shopping (flight-dependent), private airport transfer.

Enjoy your final morning in Sri Lanka. Depending on your flight time, travel towards Colombo for sightseeing or shopping. Continue to Bandaranaike International Airport for your departure. Airport drop-off included.

Your itinerary can be tailored to your pace and flight times. Optional activities, train journeys and marine excursions depend on availability, operating schedules and suitable conditions. Wildlife sightings vary.
