Your island story

4 DAYS OF DISCOVERY

Day by day

Explore each day’s experiences, stops and overnight destination.

01DAY 1Kandy to SigiriyaOvernight: Sigiriya

Route: Kandy to Matale, Dambulla, and Sigiriya

Highlights: Matale Hindu Temple, Nalanda Gedige, Dambulla Cave Temple, traditional village experience, cooking demonstration, village lunch, spice garden, and Sigiriya Lion Rock.

Begin your journey in Kandy and travel towards Sigiriya through the beautiful countryside of central Sri Lanka. Make your first cultural stop at a traditional Hindu temple in Matale to discover the island's diverse religious architecture. Continue to Nalanda Gedige, an unusual ancient stone monument blending Buddhist and Hindu artistic influences. Proceed to the Dambulla Cave Temple, a UNESCO World Heritage site containing five main shrine caves packed with ancient Buddhist paintings and statues. Next, immerse yourself in everyday rural life during a traditional village experience. Learn local cooking techniques and enjoy a freshly prepared traditional village lunch with your hosts. After visiting a traditional spice and herbal garden, spend your afternoon scaling the spectacular Sigiriya Rock Fortress, rising 200 meters above the jungle canopy.

Stay · Sigiriya
02DAY 2Sigiriya to Anuradhapura & ReturnOvernight: Sigiriya

Route: Sigiriya to Anuradhapura (Day Excursion) and back to Sigiriya

Highlights: Ancient City of Anuradhapura, sacred Sri Maha Bodhi, Ruwanwelisaya, Thuparamaya, Jetavanaramaya, Abhayagiri Monastery, and Samadhi Buddha Statue.

After breakfast, enjoy a scenic drive from Sigiriya to Anuradhapura, Sri Lanka's first great capital and a major center of ancient Buddhist civilization. Spend the day exploring a vast archaeological landscape filled with majestic monuments. Pay your respects at the sacred Sri Maha Bodhi, one of the world's oldest historically documented trees. Marvel at the brilliant white dome of Ruwanwelisaya Stupa, explore the ancient monastic ruins of Abhayagiri and Jetavanaramaya, and admire the serene expression of the classic Samadhi Buddha Statue. Return to Sigiriya in the evening to unwind.

Stay · Sigiriya
03DAY 3Sigiriya to Polonnaruwa & Elephant SafariOvernight: Sigiriya

Route: Sigiriya to Polonnaruwa and Minneriya/Kaudulla National Park

Highlights: UNESCO Ancient City of Polonnaruwa, Royal Palace, Sacred Quadrangle, Vatadage, Gal Vihara, and an evening wild elephant safari.

Travel towards Polonnaruwa, the sophisticated second medieval capital of Sri Lanka. Explore the remarkably preserved royal palace complex, the intricate stone carvings of the Sacred Quadrangle, the circular Vatadage, and the monumental rock-carved statues of the Gal Vihara, which showcase the incredible mastery of ancient stone masons. In the afternoon, head out on an exciting jeep safari through Minneriya or Kaudulla National Park, chosen based on seasonal elephant movements, to observe wild elephants and diverse birdlife in their natural habitat. Return to Sigiriya for a restful night.

Stay · Sigiriya
04DAY 4Sigiriya to Kandy

Route: Sigiriya back to Kandy

Highlights: Traditional wood-carving workshop, Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, Bahirawakanda Big Buddha, and Asgiriya Temple.

Travel back south into the hill country towards Kandy. Stop at a traditional wood-carving workshop to witness local artisans turn natural timber into intricate cultural art using generational techniques and natural color finishes. Arrive in the UNESCO-listed city of Kandy to visit the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, which houses the sacred tooth of the Buddha. Continue to the Bahirawakanda Temple for sweeping panoramic views of Kandy city and visit the historic monastic center of Asgiriya Temple. Your tour concludes in Kandy, with optional hotel drop-off or airport transfer arrangements available.

Your itinerary can be tailored to your pace and flight times. Optional activities, train journeys and marine excursions depend on availability, operating schedules and suitable conditions. Wildlife sightings vary.
