10 DAYS OF DISCOVERY

Day by day

Explore each day’s experiences, stops and overnight destination.

01DAY 1Arrival in Sri Lanka to TrincomaleeOvernight: Trincomalee

Route: Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) to Trincomalee

Highlights: Welcome to Sri Lanka, private airport transfer, scenic countryside drive, tropical coastal arrival, hotel check-in and evening relaxation.

Welcome to Sri Lanka! Meet your Loving Lanka Tours chauffeur guide right at Bandaranaike International Airport and begin your private journey towards the beautiful east coast. Travel through the picturesque Sri Lankan countryside and enjoy your first views of tropical landscapes, villages, paddy fields, and local life. Depending on your arrival time, suitable stops can be arranged for refreshments and rest during the journey. Upon arrival in Trincomalee, check in to your hotel and unwind after your flight, enjoying a peaceful evening surrounded by the tropical atmosphere of Sri Lanka's east coast.

Stay · Trincomalee
02DAY 2Explore Historic TrincomaleeOvernight: Trincomalee

Highlights: Koneswaram Temple, Swami Rock, Fort Frederick, natural harbour panoramic views, and afternoon beach relaxation.

After breakfast, enjoy a full day exploring the beautiful and historic city of Trincomalee. Begin your sightseeing with a visit to the famous Koneswaram Temple, dramatically situated above the Indian Ocean. Discover the religious and cultural importance of this historic Hindu temple while enjoying spectacular views over the coastline. Visit the area around Swami Rock and take in panoramic views of the Indian Ocean and Trincomalee's natural harbour. Continue to Fort Frederick, an important historic landmark overlooking the harbour, and learn about Trincomalee's long strategic history. In the afternoon, enjoy some relaxing time beside the ocean—choose to walk along the beach or relax at your hotel.

Stay · Trincomalee
03DAY 3Nilaveli Beach & Pigeon IslandOvernight: Trincomalee / Nilaveli

Highlights: Nilaveli Beach, soft sandy coastline, optional boat excursion to Pigeon Island National Park, snorkelling, and marine life observation.

After breakfast, enjoy a beautiful day around Nilaveli Beach, renowned for its soft sandy beach, clear tropical waters, and peaceful coastal atmosphere. Depending on sea and weather conditions, take an optional boat excursion to Pigeon Island National Park. Enjoy the opportunity to explore the surrounding marine environment and, where conditions are suitable, experience snorkelling and observe tropical marine life. After returning from the excursion, enjoy a relaxed afternoon swimming or walking along Nilaveli Beach before returning to your hotel in the evening.

Stay · Trincomalee / Nilaveli
04DAY 4Trincomalee to PasikudaOvernight: Pasikuda

Route: Trincomalee to Pasikuda

Highlights: Scenic east coast drive, local coastal villages, paddy fields, Pasikuda Beach, and resort relaxation.

After breakfast, leave Trincomalee and continue south along the scenic east coast. Travel through local villages, countryside, paddy fields, and beautiful coastal landscapes, enjoying the changing scenery as you travel towards Pasikuda. Arrive in Pasikuda, famous for its beautiful sandy coastline and shallow turquoise waters. Check in to your hotel and enjoy a relaxed afternoon spending time on the beach or enjoying the facilities at your resort.

Stay · Pasikuda
05DAY 5Pasikuda Beach LeisureOvernight: Pasikuda

Highlights: Tropical beach relaxation, swimming, coastal walks, resort leisure, and sunset views.

Enjoy a relaxing full day in Pasikuda. After several days of travelling and sightseeing, this is an opportunity to slow down and enjoy the tropical east coast. Possible activities include beach relaxation, swimming where conditions are suitable, coastal walks, resort activities, optional water activities, optional local sightseeing, and enjoying the sunset. The day can remain completely flexible according to your preferred pace.

Stay · Pasikuda
06DAY 6Pasikuda to Arugam BayOvernight: Arugam Bay

Route: Pasikuda to Arugam Bay

Highlights: Eastern countryside drive, lagoons, tropical landscapes, Arugam Bay arrival, and sunset beach walk.

After breakfast, check out from your hotel in Pasikuda and begin your journey south towards Arugam Bay. Enjoy a scenic drive through the eastern countryside, passing local villages, paddy fields, lagoons, and tropical landscapes that gradually lead towards the relaxed coastal atmosphere of Arugam Bay. Upon arrival, check in to your hotel and take some time to relax. In the evening, enjoy a relaxing visit to Arugam Bay Beach—take a peaceful walk along the coastline, enjoy the ocean views, or relax on the beach while watching the sunset. For guests interested in surfing, optional surfing activities or lessons can be arranged depending on conditions and availability.

Stay · Arugam Bay
07DAY 7Kumana National Park SafariOvernight: Arugam Bay

Highlights: Kumana National Park private jeep safari, wetland birdlife, elephants, wildlife photography, and Arugam Bay evening.

Start the day with an exciting wildlife experience at Kumana National Park. An early morning departure is arranged to make the most of the cooler hours and active wildlife. Enjoy a private jeep safari through the natural landscapes of Kumana, a park particularly known for its birdlife and wetland habitats that make it an exceptional destination for wildlife enthusiasts and photographers. Depending on natural conditions and animal movements, look out for elephants, deer, wild buffalo, crocodiles, monkeys, and different species of birds. After the safari, return to Arugam Bay to rest and spend a relaxed afternoon and evening beside the ocean.

Stay · Arugam Bay
08DAY 8Arugam Bay to Nuwara Eliya via EllaOvernight: Nuwara Eliya

Route: Arugam Bay to Ella and Nuwara Eliya

Highlights: Coast-to-mountain transition, Ella mountain town, Nine Arch Bridge, Little Adam's Peak, Ravana Falls, and tea-country ascent.

Leave Arugam Bay after breakfast and begin your journey towards the beautiful central highlands, transitioning from the tropical east coast into the cool, green mountain region. En route, visit the beautiful mountain town of Ella, surrounded by dramatic mountains, tea plantations, valleys, and waterfalls. Depending on available time, visit iconic spots such as the Nine Arch Bridge, Little Adam's Peak, or Ravana Falls. After exploring Ella, continue your scenic drive towards Nuwara Eliya, watching the landscape become cooler and greener as tea plantations cover the mountain slopes. Check in to your hotel and enjoy the peaceful hill-country evening.

Stay · Nuwara Eliya
09DAY 9Nuwara Eliya to KandyOvernight: Kandy

Route: Nuwara Eliya to Kandy

Highlights: Ceylon tea plantation and factory tour, tea tasting, waterfall stop, gem museum experience, and Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic.

Leave Nuwara Eliya after breakfast and begin your scenic journey towards Kandy, combining Sri Lanka's famous tea culture, mountain scenery, and cultural heritage. Begin with a visit to a Ceylon tea plantation and factory, walking through the tea-growing landscape to learn how tea is cultivated, processed, dried, and graded, followed by a traditional tea-tasting experience. Stop at a suitable waterfall along the route for photographs and a short break. Upon reaching the Kandy area, visit a Sri Lankan gem museum to discover traditional gemstone cutting and jewellery craftsmanship. Conclude your afternoon with a visit to the magnificent Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, one of Sri Lanka's most important Buddhist religious and cultural sites.

Stay · Kandy
10DAY 10Kandy to Airport Departure

Route: Kandy to Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB)

Highlights: Final morning in Kandy, flexible morning sightseeing, optional Pinnawala Elephant Experience, and private airport transfer.

Enjoy your final morning in Sri Lanka. After breakfast, check out from your hotel and begin your private journey from Kandy towards Bandaranaike International Airport, with activities planned according to your flight schedule. If your flight is later in the day, additional short sightseeing or cultural experiences in Kandy can be arranged. En route, an optional stop at the Pinnawala Elephant Experience can be arranged if time permits. Your chauffeur ensures a comfortable and timely transfer directly to the airport for your international departure.

Airport drop-off included.

Your itinerary can be tailored to your pace and flight times. Optional activities, train journeys and marine excursions depend on availability, operating schedules and suitable conditions. Wildlife sightings vary.
