10 DAYS OF DISCOVERY

Day by day

Explore each day’s experiences, stops and overnight destination.

01DAY 1Airport Arrival & Journey to the Cultural TriangleOvernight: Sigiriya / Dambulla

Route: Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) to Sigiriya / Dambulla

Highlights: VIP airport meet-and-greet, private air-conditioned transfer, scenic rural landscapes.

Your Sri Lankan adventure begins the moment you land at Bandaranaike International Airport. Your professional, English-speaking chauffeur guide from Loving Lanka Tours will be waiting to warmly welcome you with traditional Sri Lankan hospitality.

Settle into your private, air-conditioned vehicle and head north toward the historic Cultural Triangle. As you leave the airport zone, the scenery transitions into lush tropical countryside dotted with rural villages, swaying coconut palms, and sprawling emerald-green paddy fields. Depending on your flight schedule, your guide can arrange pleasant stops along the way for refreshing king coconuts or authentic local snacks. By evening, arrive at your hotel in Sigiriya or Dambulla, unwind, and rest up for tomorrow's monumental discoveries.

Stay · Sigiriya / Dambulla
02DAY 2Ancient Wonders: Sigiriya Rock Fortress & Dambulla Cave TempleOvernight: Sigiriya / Dambulla

Highlights: UNESCO World Heritage Sigiriya Rock Fortress, Lion’s Paw, ancient water gardens, Dambulla Cave Temple.

After a hearty breakfast, dive straight into the heart of Sri Lanka’s ancient history by visiting two monumental UNESCO World Heritage Sites.

First, head to the awe-inspiring Sigiriya Rock Fortress, a 5th-century sky palace built by King Kasyapa. Explore the symmetrical water gardens, boulder gardens, and ancient engineering marvels before beginning your ascent. Climb past the famous mirror wall and frescoes of heavenly maidens, reaching the iconic Lion’s Paw entrance before stepping onto the summit for panoramic views of the surrounding dry-zone jungles.

In the afternoon, proceed to the sacred Dambulla Cave Temple. Carved into a massive granite rock face towering over the plains, this 1st-century BCE complex consists of five pristine caves filled with more than 150 intricate Buddha statues and vibrant religious murals. It offers a profound look into the island's deep-rooted Buddhist heritage. Return to your hotel in the evening for a peaceful night.

Stay · Sigiriya / Dambulla
03DAY 3Sigiriya to Kandy via Spice Gardens & Temple of the Sacred Tooth RelicOvernight: Kandy

Highlights: Matale spice and herbal gardens, scenic transition to the central hills, Kandy Lake, Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, Kandyan cultural dance.

Bid farewell to Sigiriya as your journey winds south toward the cooler central hills. The dry-zone landscape gradually transforms into a vibrant green blanket of vegetation. En route, stop at a traditional Spice and Herbal Garden in the Matale district. Discover how world-famous Ceylon spices—including cinnamon, cardamom, pepper, cloves, and nutmeg—are grown and utilized in authentic Sri Lankan cooking and traditional Ayurvedic wellness.

Continue climbing into Kandy, the historic hill capital of Sri Lanka and a vibrant cultural hub. In the late afternoon, visit the magnificent Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic (Sri Dalada Maligawa), which houses the left canine tooth of Lord Buddha. Experience the evening pooja ceremony filled with traditional drummers and fragrant lotus flowers. Conclude your evening by taking in an energetic Kandyan Cultural Dance performance, showcasing traditional drumming, fire-walking, and colorful local costumes.

Stay · Kandy
04DAY 4Kandy to Nuwara Eliya ("Little England")Overnight: Nuwara Eliya

Highlights: Royal Botanical Gardens Peradeniya, Ceylon tea plantations and factory tour, tea tasting, Ramboda Waterfalls, Nuwara Eliya colonial town.

Begin your morning with a peaceful stroll through the Royal Botanical Gardens in Peradeniya, spanning over 147 acres and featuring thousands of tropical plants, giant bamboo groves, and a striking orchid house.

Next, ascend deeper into the central highlands. The winding roads unfold jaw-dropping vistas of misty mountains draped in emerald tea carpets. Stop at a working Ceylon Tea Plantation and Factory to trace the journey of tea from leaf to cup, complete with a guided factory walkthrough and a fresh Ceylon tea tasting session.

Resume your drive, stopping to admire the cascading waters of Ramboda Falls plunging down the mountain cliff. By afternoon, arrive in Nuwara Eliya, affectionately known as "Little England" due to its crisp mountain climate, colonial-era brick post office, Tudor-style country houses, and manicured lawns.

Stay · Nuwara Eliya
05DAY 5Nuwara Eliya to Scenic EllaOvernight: Ella

Highlights: High-altitude mountain landscapes, dramatic valley views, optional scenic hill-country train ride, relaxed evening in Ella town.

Set off on one of the most breathtaking drives in South Asia, heading from Nuwara Eliya toward the bohemian mountain village of Ella. The road snakes past cascading waterfalls, deep gorges, and uninterrupted valleys of tea estates.

Special Option: For travelers who love classic rail travel, a scenic train journey through the hill country can be arranged (subject to ticket availability and railway operations), allowing you to enjoy the views as you cross dramatic viaducts and mist-shrouded peaks.

Arrive in Ella and check into your mountain-view accommodation. Spend a relaxed evening strolling through the vibrant, laid-back Ella town, sipping local coffee at a cozy café, or simply resting while listening to the sounds of nature.

Stay · Ella
06DAY 6Ella Exploration to Wildlife Country (Yala or Udawalawe)Overnight: Yala / Udawalawe

Highlights: Nine Arch Bridge, Little Adam's Peak hike, Ravana Waterfalls, afternoon transfer for a wildlife safari.

Rise early to catch the magic of Ella's iconic landmarks. Visit the architectural marvel of the Nine Arch Bridge, a colonial-era brick-and-stone railway bridge nestled deep in dense jungle and tea fields. Time it right to watch a colorful blue train rumble across the arches. Next, embark on an easy, rewarding hike up Little Adam’s Peak for panoramic views across the sweeping Ella Gap. After catching a glimpse of the roaring Ravana Waterfall, journey south toward Sri Lanka's premier wildlife territory.

Choose between Udawalawe National Park (renowned for high concentrations of wild Asian elephants) or Yala National Park (famous for leopards, elephants, sloth bears, and rich birdlife). Arrive at your jungle lodge, check in, and rest up in anticipation of tomorrow's dawn safari.

Stay · Yala / Udawalawe
07DAY 7Morning Safari & Journey to Mirissa BeachOvernight: Mirissa

Highlights: Dawn Jeep Safari in Yala or Udawalawe National Park, scenic drive to the southern coast, golden hour at Mirissa Beach.

Awaken before dawn for an exhilarating morning wildlife safari in a 4x4 Jeep. The early hours offer optimal lighting and peak animal activity. Keep your cameras ready to spot roaming herds of wild elephants, majestic water buffalo, spotted deer, crocodiles, troop-leading monkeys, and an array of colorful exotic birds.

Following the safari, enjoy a hearty breakfast back at the hotel before heading south toward the tropical coastline. Watch the dry lowlands shift into coastal roads lined with coconut palms and golden beaches. Arrive in Mirissa, check into your seaside hotel, and spend the evening unwinding with a barefoot walk on the sand as the sun sets over the Indian Ocean.

Stay · Mirissa
08DAY 8Early Morning Whale Watching to UnawatunaOvernight: Unawatuna

Highlights: Indian Ocean whale and dolphin watching excursion, coastal transit, relaxing evening in Unawatuna.

Embark on an unforgettable early-morning whale-watching excursion off the coast of Mirissa. The southern waters of Sri Lanka are globally recognized as one of the best locations to spot majestic Blue Whales, Sperm Whales, playful pods of dolphins, and marine turtles swimming in their natural habitat.

After returning to shore for breakfast and a brief rest, continue your coastal journey westward toward Unawatuna. Unawatuna is celebrated for its horseshoe-shaped palm-fringed bay and crystal-clear turquoise waters. Spend the evening relaxing on the soft sand or enjoying a fresh seafood dinner by the waves.

Stay · Unawatuna
09DAY 9Unawatuna Beach Bliss & Historic Galle FortOvernight: Galle / Unawatuna

Highlights: Morning beach leisure, optional snorkeling, afternoon exploration of UNESCO Galle Fort, atmospheric sunset rampart walk, farewell dinner.

Savor a slow, relaxing morning on Unawatuna Beach. Take a refreshing swim or choose an optional snorkeling experience in the vibrant coastal waters to view marine life. The morning schedule remains entirely flexible to match your mood—whether you prefer lounging under a palm tree or exploring hidden local coves like Jungle Beach.

In the late afternoon, travel a short distance to the historic Galle Fort, a UNESCO World Heritage site initially built by the Portuguese in the 16th century and fortified by the Dutch. Walk through cobblestone streets lined with colonial-era villas, chic boutique shops, art galleries, and hip cafés. Walk along the ancient stone ramparts to watch a breathtaking sunset over the Indian Ocean. Celebrate your final night in Sri Lanka with a special farewell dinner.

Stay · Galle / Unawatuna
10DAY 10Galle to Bandaranaike International Airport (Departure)

Highlights: Leisurely breakfast, final coastal moments, private airport transfer.

Enjoy your final morning in tropical Sri Lanka at a relaxed pace. Relish a delicious breakfast at your hotel, take one last walk along the beach, or pick up a few final souvenirs.

Your professional Loving Lanka Tours chauffeur will meet you at the hotel lobby and provide a smooth, comfortable private transfer directly to Bandaranaike International Airport in time for your international departure flight. Leave the island with a camera full of memories and a heart full of authentic Sri Lankan experiences.

Airport drop-off included.

Your itinerary can be tailored to your pace and flight times. Optional activities, train journeys and marine excursions depend on availability, operating schedules and suitable conditions. Wildlife sightings vary.
