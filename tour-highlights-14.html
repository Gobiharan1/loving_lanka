14 DAYS OF DISCOVERY

Day by day

Explore each day’s experiences, stops and overnight destination.

01DAY 1Arrival in Sri LankaOvernight: Anuradhapura

Route: Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) to Anuradhapura

Highlights: Welcome to Sri Lanka, private transfer, journey to the Cultural Triangle, hotel check-in.

Welcome to Sri Lanka! Meet your Loving Lanka Tours chauffeur guide at Bandaranaike International Airport. Begin your journey towards the Cultural Triangle and settle into your hotel. Depending on your arrival time, enjoy a relaxed evening or an optional local experience.

Stay · Anuradhapura
02DAY 2Explore AnuradhapuraOvernight: Anuradhapura

Highlights: Ancient kingdom of Anuradhapura, sacred Sri Maha Bodhi, ancient stupas, monasteries, historic ruins.

Discover the ancient kingdom of Anuradhapura, one of Sri Lanka’s most important historical and religious destinations. Visit the sacred Sri Maha Bodhi, ancient stupas, monasteries and historic ruins. Experience the atmosphere of one of Sri Lanka’s oldest royal capitals and learn about the island’s fascinating Buddhist heritage.

Stay · Anuradhapura
03DAY 3Anuradhapura to PolonnaruwaOvernight: Polonnaruwa / Habarana

Highlights: Ancient city of Polonnaruwa, archaeological ruins, royal palace complex, Gal Vihara granite statues.

Travel towards the ancient city of Polonnaruwa. Explore the impressive archaeological ruins, ancient royal palace complex, temples and the famous Gal Vihara, renowned for its magnificent Buddha statues carved from granite. Enjoy a relaxed evening surrounded by the beautiful countryside.

Stay · Polonnaruwa / Habarana
04DAY 4Polonnaruwa to SigiriyaOvernight: Sigiriya / Dambulla

Highlights: Traditional Sri Lankan village experience, local life, traditional cooking, rural surroundings.

Continue towards Sigiriya. Enjoy a traditional Sri Lankan village experience, with opportunities to experience local life, traditional cooking and the beautiful rural surroundings. Later, relax at your hotel or enjoy an optional sunset experience.

Stay · Sigiriya / Dambulla
05DAY 5Sigiriya & DambullaOvernight: Sigiriya / Dambulla

Highlights: UNESCO World Heritage Sigiriya Rock Fortress, historic gardens, fortress ruins, Dambulla Cave Temple.

Visit the spectacular Sigiriya Rock Fortress, one of Sri Lanka’s most famous ancient sites. Explore the historic gardens, fortress ruins and surrounding landscapes. Later, visit the magnificent Dambulla Cave Temple, famous for its ancient murals and Buddha statues. Enjoy a relaxing evening at your hotel.

Stay · Sigiriya / Dambulla
06DAY 6Sigiriya to KandyOvernight: Kandy

Highlights: Cultural capital transition, traditional spice garden, Ayurvedic traditions, Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, Kandyan cultural performance.

Travel towards the cultural capital of Sri Lanka. Visit a traditional spice garden and discover Sri Lanka’s spices and Ayurvedic traditions. Continue to Kandy and visit the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic. In the evening, enjoy a traditional cultural performance if available.

Stay · Kandy
07DAY 7Explore KandyOvernight: Kandy

Highlights: Royal Botanical Garden Peradeniya, Kandy city tour, local handicrafts, gems, free time.

Spend the day discovering beautiful Kandy. Visit the Royal Botanical Garden in Peradeniya, explore the city and discover local handicrafts, gems and Sri Lankan culture. Enjoy some free time to relax or explore the city at your own pace.

Stay · Kandy
08DAY 8Kandy to Nuwara EliyaOvernight: Nuwara Eliya

Highlights: Central highlands, mountain views, valleys, waterfalls, Ceylon tea plantation and factory tour, Nuwara Eliya ("Little England").

Travel through the spectacular central highlands. Enjoy breathtaking views of mountains, valleys and waterfalls. Visit a tea plantation and factory and learn how world-famous Ceylon Tea is produced. Continue to Nuwara Eliya, known as Little England, and enjoy its cool climate and colonial atmosphere.

Stay · Nuwara Eliya
09DAY 9Nuwara Eliya to EllaOvernight: Ella

Highlights: Scenic hill country, tea plantations, mountain landscapes, optional scenic train journey.

Continue through the scenic hill country towards Ella. Enjoy spectacular views of tea plantations and mountain landscapes. A scenic train experience can be arranged subject to railway operation and ticket availability. Arrive in Ella and enjoy a relaxing evening.

Stay · Ella
10DAY 10Explore EllaOvernight: Ella

Highlights: Nine Arch Bridge, tea-covered mountains, Ravana Waterfall, flexible outdoor activities.

Discover the beautiful mountain town of Ella. Visit the famous Nine Arch Bridge and enjoy spectacular views of the surrounding tea-covered mountains. Continue to Ravana Waterfall and explore the local area. The day’s activities can be adjusted according to your interests and fitness level.

Stay · Ella
11DAY 11Ella to Yala / UdawalaweOvernight: Yala / Udawalawe

Highlights: Wildlife region transfer, Yala or Udawalawe National Park safari, wild elephants, diverse wildlife.

Travel towards Sri Lanka’s wildlife region. Depending on your preferred route, visit Yala National Park or Udawalawe National Park. Enjoy a wildlife safari and look for elephants, crocodiles, deer, monkeys and many species of birds.

Stay · Yala / Udawalawe
12DAY 12Safari & South CoastOvernight: South Coast / Unawatuna / Galle

Highlights: Early morning wildlife safari, transition to the southern coastline, Indian Ocean beach relaxation.

Enjoy an early morning wildlife safari. After breakfast, continue towards Sri Lanka’s beautiful southern coastline. Arrive at your beach hotel and spend the afternoon relaxing beside the Indian Ocean.

Stay · South Coast / Unawatuna / Galle
13DAY 13Galle & Southern CoastOvernight: Galle / Unawatuna / South Coast

Highlights: Historic Galle Fort, colonial architecture, narrow streets, boutique cafés, free time at the beach, optional excursions.

Explore the historic Galle Fort, famous for its colonial architecture, charming streets, cafés, boutiques and ocean views. Continue along the southern coast and enjoy some free time at the beach. Optional experiences can include a boat trip, turtle experience, local village visit or sunset by the ocean.

Stay · Galle / Unawatuna / South Coast
14DAY 14South Coast to Colombo / Airport

Highlights: Final morning, beach relaxation or Colombo shopping/sightseeing (flight-dependent), private airport transfer.

Enjoy your final morning in Sri Lanka. Depending on your flight time, relax at the beach or enjoy a short Colombo sightseeing and shopping experience. Transfer to Bandaranaike International Airport for your departure. Airport drop-off included.

Your itinerary can be tailored to your pace and flight times. Optional activities, train journeys and marine excursions depend on availability, operating schedules and suitable conditions. Wildlife sightings vary.
