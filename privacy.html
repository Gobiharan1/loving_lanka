Privacy & Your Inquiry

How the trip planner handles the information you share.

The trip planner

The planner prepares an inquiry on your device using your name, email address, optional phone number, travel dates, duration, group size, accommodation tier, package preference, interests and message. This website does not send these details to a server or store them.

Choosing WhatsApp or email

After you review your message, you can open WhatsApp or an email draft. The prepared details are included in the link for your chosen service. That provider receives the message text when its page or application opens. Loving Lanka Tours receives your inquiry when you send it in that application. Share only what is needed to discuss your trip.

External services

This site uses Google Fonts to load typefaces. Choosing German or French in the language selector opens a Google Translate version of the current public page. Google receives that page URL and provides the translation under its own privacy practices. Your browser may contact Google to request them. WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Tripadvisor and Unsplash are external services with their own privacy practices. No analytics scripts or tracking cookies are added by this website.
