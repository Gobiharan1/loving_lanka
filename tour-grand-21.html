21 DAYS OF DISCOVERY

Day by day

Explore each day’s experiences, stops and overnight destination.

01DAY 1Airport to AnuradhapuraOvernight: Anuradhapura

Route: Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) to Anuradhapura

Highlights: Welcome to Sri Lanka, private transfer, ancient northern cultural region, rural landscapes, villages, paddy fields, tropical surroundings, hotel check-in.

Welcome to Sri Lanka! Meet your Loving Lanka Tours chauffeur guide at Bandaranaike International Airport and begin your private journey towards Anuradhapura. Travel through the Sri Lankan countryside towards the island's ancient northern cultural region, giving you your first look at rural landscapes, villages, paddy fields and tropical surroundings. Depending on your international flight arrival time, stops can be arranged for refreshments and rest. Arrive in Anuradhapura, check in to your hotel and relax after your flight. Anuradhapura was one of Sri Lanka's great ancient capitals and an important centre of Buddhist civilization containing stupas, monasteries, temples, sculptures, reservoirs and archaeological remains. Keep the first evening relaxed so you can enjoy the following day's full cultural experience.

Stay · Anuradhapura
02DAY 2Anuradhapura Ancient CityOvernight: Anuradhapura

Highlights: Full day exploring the magnificent Ancient City of Anuradhapura, sacred Sri Maha Bodhi, Ruwanwelisaya Stupa, Thuparamaya, Jetavanaramaya, Abhayagiri Monastery Complex, Samadhi Buddha Statue, ancient ponds and irrigation heritage.

After breakfast, spend the full day exploring the magnificent Ancient City of Anuradhapura, which served as Sri Lanka's capital for many centuries and remains one of the island's most important sacred and archaeological destinations. Begin with the sacred Sri Maha Bodhi, one of the most important Buddhist pilgrimage sites in Sri Lanka, and experience the peaceful religious atmosphere. Continue to the magnificent Ruwanwelisaya and Thuparamaya stupas, explore the massive Jetavanaramaya monument, and discover the extensive Abhayagiri Monastery Complex. Visit the peaceful Samadhi Buddha Statue, renowned for its serene expression, and explore ancient ponds and sophisticated irrigation systems that supported ancient urban life. Return to your hotel after a full day of exploration.

Stay · Anuradhapura
03DAY 3Anuradhapura to Nilaveli / TrincomaleeOvernight: Trincomalee / Nilaveli

Highlights: Transition from ancient heritage to the tropical east coast, scenic drive, Trincomalee region, Nilaveli Beach, Indian Ocean relaxation.

After breakfast, leave Anuradhapura and travel towards the beautiful east coast of Sri Lanka. As you travel east, the scenery gradually changes from the dry-zone cultural landscape to tropical coastal surroundings. Arrive in the Trincomalee region and continue to Nilaveli Beach, famous for its beautiful sandy coastline and tropical surroundings. Enjoy time beside the Indian Ocean and relax after your cultural experiences in Anuradhapura, taking in the more relaxed coastal environment of the east coast.

Stay · Trincomalee / Nilaveli
04DAY 4TrincomaleeOvernight: Trincomalee / Nilaveli

Highlights: Koneswaram Temple, Swami Rock panoramic views, historic Fort Frederick, Nilaveli Beach.

Enjoy a full day exploring the Trincomalee area. Visit the famous Koneswaram Temple, dramatically positioned above the ocean, and discover its history and religious importance while enjoying spectacular coastline views. Visit Swami Rock for panoramic views over the Indian Ocean and explore the historic Fort Frederick area to learn about Trincomalee's long strategic history as a natural harbour. Finish the day with relaxing time at Nilaveli Beach, adjusting the schedule according to whether you prefer sightseeing or time by the sea.

Stay · Trincomalee / Nilaveli
05DAY 5Trincomalee to PasikudaOvernight: Pasikuda

Highlights: Scenic east coast journey, rural villages, paddy fields, tropical landscapes, Pasikuda beach arrival.

After breakfast, begin your journey south along Sri Lanka's east coast, traveling through rural villages, paddy fields, tropical landscapes and coastal communities. Arrive at Pasikuda, known for its beautiful sandy coastline and shallow coastal waters. Check in to your hotel, enjoy a relaxed afternoon on the beach, or simply take advantage of the resort facilities.

Stay · Pasikuda
06DAY 6PasikudaOvernight: Pasikuda

Highlights: Tropical east coast beach relaxation, swimming, coastal walks, resort activities, flexible schedule.

Enjoy a relaxed full day in Pasikuda. After several days of cultural exploration, this is an opportunity to slow down and enjoy the tropical coastline. Possible activities include beach relaxation, swimming where conditions are suitable, coastal walks, resort activities, optional water activities or optional local sightseeing according to your preferred pace.

Stay · Pasikuda
07DAY 7Pasikuda to Polonnaruwa & SigiriyaOvernight: Sigiriya

Highlights: Ancient City of Polonnaruwa, royal palace complex, Audience Hall, Sacred Quadrangle (Dalada Maluwa), Vatadage, Rankoth Vehera, Lankatilaka, Gal Vihara granite statues, Parakrama Samudra, transfer to Sigiriya.

After breakfast, leave Pasikuda and travel towards the ancient kingdom of Polonnaruwa, Sri Lanka's second great medieval capital. Explore impressive archaeological ruins, ancient royal buildings, the royal palace complex, the Audience Hall, and the Sacred Quadrangle. Admire the circular design of the Polonnaruwa Vatadage, visit Rankoth Vehera and Lankatilaka, and view the magnificent seated, standing and reclining Buddha statues carved directly into the granite rock face at Gal Vihara. See the massive Parakrama Samudra irrigation reservoir before continuing towards Sigiriya for your evening stay.

Stay · Sigiriya
08DAY 8SigiriyaOvernight: Sigiriya

Highlights: UNESCO World Heritage Sigiriya Rock Fortress, historic gardens, frescoes, optional Pidurangala Rock hike, traditional Sri Lankan village experience, local cooking experience, traditional village lunch.

Enjoy a full day exploring the Sigiriya region. Visit the spectacular Sigiriya Rock Fortress, exploring ancient gardens, water-management features, and famous frescoes, with an optional hike to Pidurangala Rock available for panoramic views. Later, visit a traditional village to experience rural Sri Lankan life, home gardens, and local farming. Take part in a traditional Sri Lankan cooking experience to learn about local spices and cooking techniques, followed by a freshly prepared traditional village lunch. Return to your hotel for a relaxing evening.

Stay · Sigiriya
09DAY 9Sigiriya to KandyOvernight: Kandy

Highlights: Dambulla Cave Temple UNESCO World Heritage Site, Nalanda Gedige stone monument (Buddhist-Hindu architecture), Matale Hindu Temple, scenic transition to the central hills, Kandy arrival.

After breakfast, leave Sigiriya and travel towards Kandy through an itinerary rich in cultural and architectural stops. First, visit the magnificent Dambulla Cave Temple, Sri Lanka's largest and best-preserved cave complex containing five shrine caves with ancient Buddhist paintings and statues. Continue to Nalanda Gedige, an unusual ancient stone monument blending Buddhist and Hindu artistic influences, then visit a traditional Hindu temple in Matale to experience the island's religious diversity. Travel through the green landscapes around Matale into the central hills, arriving in the historic hill capital of Kandy for a relaxed evening.

Stay · Kandy
10DAY 10Kandy City TourOvernight: Kandy

Highlights: UNESCO World Heritage city Kandy, Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, Bahirawakanda Big Buddha, Kandy Lake and Viewpoint, gem cutting experience, traditional wood-carving workshop, spice and herbal experience, Kandyan cultural dance performance.

Spend a full day exploring Kandy, the cultural capital of Sri Lanka. Visit the sacred Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic within the former royal palace complex, and travel up to Bahirawakanda Temple for panoramic views from the Big Buddha statue. Enjoy scenic views around Kandy Lake and the city viewpoint. Experience local craftsmanship by visiting a traditional gemstone cutting and jewellery workshop, and a wood-carving workshop where local artisans demonstrate traditional hand tools and natural finishes. Learn about local spices and herbs, and conclude your evening with a traditional Kandyan cultural dance performance featuring vibrant music and costumes.

Stay · Kandy
11DAY 11Kandy to Nuwara EliyaOvernight: Nuwara Eliya

Highlights: Central highlands, Ceylon tea plantation and factory tour, tea-tasting experience, Ramboda Waterfalls, Nuwara Eliya "Little England" cool climate and colonial atmosphere.

After breakfast, leave Kandy and travel into the breathtaking central highlands. Visit a working tea plantation and factory to learn how world-famous Ceylon Tea is cultivated, harvested, and processed, and enjoy a traditional tea-tasting experience. Stop at the stunning Ramboda Waterfalls to take in the mountain scenery. Continue your journey to Nuwara Eliya, enjoying its cool climate, beautiful valleys, and charming colonial-era architecture.

Stay · Nuwara Eliya
12DAY 12Nuwara Eliya to EllaOvernight: Ella

Highlights: Scenic hill country, lush mountain landscapes, tea estates, waterfalls, optional scenic train journey, arrival in Ella.

After breakfast, travel from Nuwara Eliya towards the mountain town of Ella. Travel through some of Sri Lanka's most spectacular mountain landscapes surrounded by tea plantations, valleys, and forests. Depending on your preference and ticket availability, a famous scenic hill-country train journey can be included along this route. Arrive in Ella and spend a relaxing evening enjoying the peaceful mountain atmosphere.

Stay · Ella
13DAY 13EllaOvernight: Ella

Highlights: Nine Arch Bridge, Little Adam's Peak hike, Ravana Falls, free time in Ella town.

Enjoy a full day discovering Ella's iconic natural and architectural landmarks. Visit the famous Nine Arch Bridge set amid lush green tea-country hills, enjoy an optional hike up Little Adam's Peak for panoramic valley views, and visit the roaring Ravana Falls. Spend your afternoon exploring Ella town's relaxed cafés, restaurants, and local shops at your own pace.

Stay · Ella
14DAY 14Ella to Udawalawe or YalaOvernight: Udawalawe / Yala area

Highlights: Transition from mountain country to wildlife country, choice of Udawalawe National Park or Yala National Park safari base, hotel check-in and rest.

After breakfast, leave the cool mountains of Ella and travel south towards Sri Lanka's premier wildlife regions. Choose between Udawalawe National Park (renowned for wild elephants) or Yala National Park (famous for diverse wildlife and leopards), selected according to your route and current conditions. Arrive at your safari destination area and relax in preparation for the next morning's safari adventure.

Stay · Udawalawe / Yala area
15DAY 15Wildlife Safari to MirissaOvernight: Mirissa

Highlights: Early morning national park jeep safari, wild elephants and dry-zone wildlife viewing, transition to the southern coastline, Mirissa beach relaxation.

Wake up early for an exciting jeep safari through your chosen national park. Explore the rich dry-zone ecosystem during the cool morning hours, with opportunities to observe wild elephants, buffalo, deer, crocodiles, and various bird species. After the safari and breakfast, continue south towards the scenic coastline, arriving in the popular beach destination of Mirissa to relax beside the Indian Ocean.

Stay · Mirissa
16DAY 16MirissaOvernight: Mirissa

Highlights: Tropical beach relaxation, coastal walks, optional whale and dolphin watching excursion, Coconut Tree Hill, local seafood.

Enjoy a full day at leisure in Mirissa. Relax on the beach, swim where conditions are suitable, take a coastal walk to Coconut Tree Hill, or enjoy local seafood. Optional morning whale and dolphin watching boat excursions can be arranged, subject to seasonal sea conditions and availability.

Stay · Mirissa
17DAY 17Mirissa to Galle & UnawatunaOvernight: Unawatuna

Highlights: Coastal drive, UNESCO World Heritage Galle Fort, colonial-era architecture, historic ramparts, boutique cafés, Unawatuna beach.

After breakfast, travel along the southern coastline towards the historic city of Galle. Explore the UNESCO-listed Galle Fort, walking through atmospheric cobblestone streets lined with old colonial buildings, churches, boutique shops, and ocean-view ramparts. Afterward, continue a short distance to Unawatuna, check in to your beach hotel, and enjoy a peaceful evening by the coast.

Stay · Unawatuna
18DAY 18UnawatunaOvernight: Unawatuna

Highlights: Free day at Unawatuna beach, swimming, snorkelling, Jungle Beach, Japanese Peace Pagoda, local cafés.

Enjoy a free day to relax or explore around Unawatuna at your own preferred pace. Choose from beach relaxation, swimming, snorkelling, visiting the scenic Jungle Beach, or taking a short trip up to the Japanese Peace Pagoda for coastal views. Enjoy the local beach restaurants and cafés in the evening.

Stay · Unawatuna
19DAY 19Unawatuna to BentotaOvernight: Bentota

Highlights: Southwestern coastal drive, scenic beach towns, arrival in Bentota.

After breakfast, check out and travel north along the picturesque southwestern coastline towards Bentota. Enjoy the coastal views and optional brief stops along the way before arriving in Bentota, a premier beach resort destination known for its wide sandy shores and river estuaries. Check in and spend a relaxing afternoon.

Stay · Bentota
20DAY 20BentotaOvernight: Bentota

Highlights: Final full day relaxation, Bentota River boat safari, mangrove tour, water sports, beach leisure.

Spend your final full day in Sri Lanka relaxing in Bentota or experiencing optional activities such as a boat ride along the Bentota River, a mangrove wildlife tour, water sports, or local sightseeing. This is your perfect opportunity to unwind by the pool or ocean before your departure.

Stay · Bentota
21DAY 21Bentota to Airport

Highlights: Final morning beach relaxation, private airport transfer, departure from Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB).

Enjoy a relaxed breakfast at your hotel and spend your final morning soaking up the coastal atmosphere, depending on your flight schedule. Your Loving Lanka Tours chauffeur will pick you up from the hotel and provide a comfortable, private air-conditioned transfer to Bandaranaike International Airport, timed precisely to meet your international flight departure. Your unforgettable 21-day grand tour of Sri Lanka comes to an end.

Airport drop-off included.

Your itinerary can be tailored to your pace and flight times. Optional activities, train journeys and marine excursions depend on availability, operating schedules and suitable conditions. Wildlife sightings vary.
